### D. Definitions to be able to state in the methods (and to tell Hugo)

Ruben asked on 23 October to keep this text for the methods section and the meeting. How each
colouring (descriptor) is measured. All are computed by `pearlatent.descriptors` on the 128³
volumes, in grid voxels, so they are relative: each fruit was rescaled to 128 voxels in length.

- **Volume:** threshold at 0.1 of the min-max-scaled intensity, keep the largest connected
  part, fill enclosed holes, count voxels.
- **Body:** the same, minus the stalk, using the same body mask as the latent features.
- **Stalk length:** slices of fruit beyond the end of the body, where a body slice has at least
  4 latent cells. **Stalk present:** stalk length of at least 10 voxels.
- **Flat face:** RANSAC (random sample consensus) plane search over the surface voxels: a plane
  through 3 random surface voxels, the surface voxels within 1 voxel of it counted, 3,000
  planes, seed 369; the largest connected patch on the best plane, divided by all surface
  voxels. A screen for cut volumes only; the eye decides.

Not size in millimetres: that needs the original length of every fruit (open question 18).
