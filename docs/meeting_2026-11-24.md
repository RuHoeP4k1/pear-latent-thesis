## 4b. Plan for the fruit dimensions (Q5)

Not for the preprocessing: Hugo said no reprocessing (question 33, 29 October), and
re-encoding would change what the encoder sees. The numbers are a measurement used beside the
volumes.

What follows from one scale factor per fruit (if the rescaling is the same along all axes):
voxel size after rescaling = original length (mm) / 128, so body volume (mm³) = body voxels ×
(length / 128)³, and stalk length in mm likewise. Example: a fruit 96 mm long including the
stalk has 0.75 mm voxels; 150,000 body voxels are then 63 cm³.
