## 3. Where it stops: grade 1

- Grade 0 against grade 1 hardly separates on any row (0.59 to 0.72), nor grade 2 against 3;
  grade 1 against 2 does (0.76 to 0.88). Not caused by the `binary_23` target: PLS on the grade
  or on `binary_123` does no better (methods section 9). Ordinal model: no grade 1 fruit
  assigned to grade 1 (parked).
- One number per fruit, no model (notebook 15): the SD of the interior cells (outer layer
  removed). Grade 0 against 1, cavity-free: CT at 32³ 0.82 (0.74 to 0.90), latent 0.50,
  encoder SD 0.57. So grade 1 is visible in the CT at 32³ and lost in the latent.
- iPLS: the information sits at mid height (about 30 to 50 % from the calyx end) and mid depth,
  for latent and CT alike.
